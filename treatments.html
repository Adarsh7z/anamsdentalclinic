Dental Treatments at Dr. Anam's Dental

Every dental treatment for adults and children under one roof in Bolarum, Secunderabad, near Kompally: from check-ups and root canals to braces, implants and full mouth rehabilitation.

Dr. Anam's Dental is a complete dental clinic. It is led by Dr. Chandra Sekar Anam, MDS, a senior prosthodontist and implantologist with over 30 years of experience, and every treatment is carried out by a dentist trained in that branch of dentistry. Whatever you need, from a child's first check-up to a wisdom tooth removal or a full set of new teeth, you can have it in one clinic, with one team that knows your history.

Every treatment we offer

  • Dental check-ups and oral examinations

  • Teeth cleaning: scaling and polishing
  • Deep cleaning (root planing)

  • Full mouth rehabilitation

A specialist for each branch

  • Prosthodontics: crowns, bridges, dentures, implants and full mouth rehabilitation (Dr. Anam's own specialty)
  • Endodontics: root canal treatment and re-treatment
  • Orthodontics: braces, clear aligners and retainers
  • Paediatric dentistry: dental care for babies, children and teenagers
  • Periodontics: gum disease treatment and deep cleaning
  • Oral and maxillofacial surgery: extractions and impacted wisdom teeth
