The cost of dental implants in Hyderabad varies depending on individual clinical factors, including the number of missing teeth, jawbone density and whether bone grafting or sinus lifting is required, the implant system selected, and the material of the final crown or bridge (such as monolithic zirconia or E-max ceramic). Because every patient's biological anatomy and treatment scope are unique, a comprehensive clinical evaluation and 3D scan are conducted to provide a transparent, personalized treatment plan and cost estimate.
While general dentists manage routine oral healthcare such as cleanings, simple fillings, and basic extractions, a prosthodontist is a dental specialist who has completed 3 years of rigorous post-graduate residency training (MDS) specifically dedicated to the restoration and replacement of teeth. Prosthodontists specialize in complex bite reconstruction (occlusion), full-mouth rehabilitation, dental implants, precision crowns, and advanced dentures. Learn more on our Prosthodontics page.
No referral is needed. While many dental practitioners across Hyderabad and Telangana refer their complex restorative and failed-treatment cases to Dr. Anam, patients can schedule a consultation directly by calling 040-27638169.
Full mouth rehabilitation is a customized, multi-phase treatment. The timeline typically ranges from a few weeks to several months depending on whether dental implants, orthodontic coordination, gum healing, or preliminary bite equilibration are needed. Dr. Anam plans each stage systematically so you maintain aesthetics and functional chewing throughout the treatment journey. See our Full Mouth Rehabilitation overview.
Dental implant placement is performed under gentle, precise local anesthesia, ensuring you remain completely comfortable and pain-free during the procedure. Most patients report that the procedure is comparable to or easier than a standard tooth extraction. Any mild soreness during the initial 48 to 72 hours after surgery is comfortably managed with prescribed routine pain relievers.
