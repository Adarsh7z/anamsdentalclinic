Specialist Dental Care Right in the Kompally Corridor

Dr. Chandra Sekar Anam's dental suite is on the 6th floor of the Fairmount building on Dulapally Road in Sri Ganesh Nagar — inside the same Kompally–Bolarum–Secunderabad corridor most patients already call home. For residents of Kompally, reaching a specialist prosthodontist doesn't mean a trip into central Hyderabad: the clinic is directly reachable via NH-44, with TSRTC bus routes connecting Kompally to Secunderabad and Koti, and Bolarum railway station roughly 4 km away (with a Metro Phase II extension toward Bolarum and Medchal planned to improve access further).

Most general dental clinics in fast-growing residential areas like Kompally are staffed by general dentists. Dr. Anam is a Professor of Prosthodontics and former National Vice President of the Indian Prosthodontic Society — the kind of specialist-level, academically grounded care that's typically only found in central Hyderabad, now available directly in the Kompally–Dulapally corridor.

Specialisations Available Locally

Our suite provides comprehensive, evidence-based restorations without the need for multi-clinic referrals:

  • Dental Implants (All-on-4/6): Permanent, biological tooth replacement anchored with micron precision and custom prosthetics.
  • Prosthodontics: Master-level diagnosis and reconstruction of complex bites, missing teeth, and functional aesthetics.
  • Advanced Restorative Dentistry: Whole-mouth restorative treatment for heavily worn, eroded, or cracked natural dentition.
  • Precision Crowns & Bridges: Micro-fitted monolithic zirconia and ceramic restorations engineered for lifetime durability.
  • Full Mouth Rehabilitation: Multi-stage reconstruction restoring chewing dynamics, bite height, and youthful facial support.
