Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Synchronized whole-mouth reconstruction rebuilding chewing dynamics, vertical height, and facial aesthetics led by Professor Dr. Chandra Sekar Anam.

Complete Whole-Mouth Reconstruction

Full mouth rehabilitation rebuilds all or most of the teeth in both arches — combining implants, crowns, bridges, and bite correction into a single coordinated treatment plan. It's typically needed after severe tooth wear, extensive tooth loss, or long-term untreated dental problems that have affected how the jaw functions. This multi-stage reconstruction is available to patients throughout Kompally, Dulapally, and greater Secunderabad, coordinated personally by Dr. Anam from consultation through final delivery (serving Kompally & Dulapally).

The Specialist Advantage in Complex Cases

This is complex, multi-stage treatment that benefits from a specialist who can plan the case as a whole system rather than tooth-by-tooth. Dr. Anam's prosthodontic training and decades of case experience are specifically suited to this kind of reconstruction.

Phases of Full Mouth Rehabilitation

  • 1. Comprehensive Occlusal Analysis: Evaluating TMJ joint kinematics, jaw arc trajectory, facial aesthetics, and bone foundation.
  • 2. Digital Smile & Bite Simulation: Creating precise diagnostic wax-ups and provisional restorations to verify comfort before final fabrication.
  • 3. Foundational & Surgical Phase: Strategic placement of implants, bone stabilization, and periodontal health optimization.
  • 4. Definitive Ceramic Restorations: Delivery of monolithic crowns, bridges, and full-arch prostheses balanced to your natural jaw motion.
