COSMETIC DENTISTRY

Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Makeovers in Secunderabad

Teeth whitening, veneers, tooth-coloured bonding and smile design, planned around your face, your bite and the long-term health of your teeth.

A natural-looking smile that lasts

Good cosmetic dentistry does not look "done". It works with the shape of your face, the line of your lips and the way you bite, so the result looks natural and stays healthy for years. Smile design is a core part of prosthodontics, Dr. Anam's own specialty, so every cosmetic plan is checked for function as well as looks.

Treatments

  • Teeth whitening: Professional whitening, supervised by a dentist, lightens teeth stained by tea, coffee, tobacco or age.
  • Veneers: Thin ceramic or composite shells bonded to the front of teeth to change their colour, shape or size.
  • Composite bonding: Tooth-coloured material shaped onto a tooth to repair chips and close small gaps, often in one visit.
  • Ceramic crowns: Full-coverage crowns for teeth that are heavily filled, broken or badly discoloured.
  • Smile makeover: A combined plan that may bring together whitening, veneers, crowns, orthodontics or implants.

Planning comes first

Before any cosmetic work, the dentist checks that your teeth and gums are healthy, because whitening or veneers placed over decay or gum disease will not last. Digital scans help plan the result and show you what to expect before treatment starts.
