EXPERT PROSTHODONTIC & IMPLANT CARE

Core Specialisations

Whether reconstructing a single missing tooth or rehabilitating an entire collapsed bite, Dr. Anam integrates custom prosthetic artistry with biomimetic engineering. Patients travel from across Kompally, Dulapally, Jeedimetla, and Secunderabad for these five specialist treatments (view Kompally clinic info or browse patient FAQs).

Dental Implants

Precision titanium and biocompatible zirconia roots anchored firmly into jawbone to replace missing teeth with natural chewing strength.

  • Single tooth precision implants
  • Full mouth rehabilitation (All-on-4 / All-on-6)
  • Custom implant-supported prostheses
  • 3D CBCT digital surgical planning
Explore Dental Implants

Prosthodontics

The premier dental specialty focused on the custom design, manufacture, and fitting of artificial replacements for teeth and gums.

  • Custom crowns & bridges
  • Complete & high-precision partial dentures
  • Full smile rehabilitation & rejuvenation
  • Overdentures & attachment-retained prosthetics
Explore Prosthodontics

Advanced Restorative

Comprehensive treatment protocols for severely worn, eroded, or traumatized teeth that require functional joint equilibrium.

  • Functional & aesthetic rehabilitation
  • Complex bite correction & vertical opening
  • TMJ harmony & occlusal equilibration
  • Biomimetic bonding & enamel preservation
Explore Restorative Care

Crowns & Bridges

Bespoke ceramic and monolithic restorations crafted under high magnification to withstand decades of daily chewing load.

  • Precision-driven microscopic margins
  • Monolithic Zirconia & E-max layering
  • Long-term durability & gum health focus
  • Natural color matching & translucency
Explore Crowns & Bridges

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Complete reconstruction of both dental arches combining implants, crowns, and occlusal realignment into a single coordinated plan.

  • Whole-mouth synchronized treatment planning
  • Severely collapsed bite elevation
  • Multi-disciplinary prosthetic reconstruction
  • Decades-long stability and masticatory comfort
Explore Full Mouth Rehab
